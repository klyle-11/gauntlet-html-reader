Our studio

A long table, two presses, a lot of thread.

Pip started as a stack of hand-stitched notebooks for friends who couldn't find a dot grid they liked. Now there are five of us, and we still fold, sew and trim every book at the same table.

We print our covers one color at a time, which is why every design is navy and cream — and why we got so fond of dots.

— Nell & the Pip studio
